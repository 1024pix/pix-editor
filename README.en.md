===

Overview
--------

[PIX](https://pix.fr) is aimed at all French-speaking citizens (students, professionals, dropouts, job seekers, seniors, citizens) who want to measure, develop and showcase their digital skills.

The service takes the form of an online platform for assessing and certifying digital skills.

The goal of PIX is to inspire lifelong learning by offering innovative, rigorous and supportive assessment methods, as well as targeted training recommendations.

Pix Editor provides an interface to read and modify the educational content repository.
